# Chapter 1: Men's Singles — Speed vs Control

## 1.1 Elite Profile Card

```
MS Elite = Footwork × Swing Speed × Tactical Execution
  Footwork: Cover any position in 2-3 steps
  Swing speed: Racket contact to ball out < 0.15s
  Tactical execution: Pre-planned strategy works in matches
```

### Elite Player Comparison

| Player | Style | Key Strength | Training Signature |
|:-------|:------|:-------------|:-------------------|
| **Lin Dan** | Control | Backhand transition + forehand attack | 2hr+ video analysis daily |
| **Lee CW** | Attack | Net pressure + smash | 100 slow-motion swings/day |
| **Chen Long** | Defense | Height + reach coverage | 40-shot rally drills |
| **Axelsen** | Attack | High smash point + coverage | 80% power philosophy |
| **Momota** | Speed | Fastest swing <0.12s | Reactive shot training |

## 1.2 Progression Path (5 Levels)

```
L1: Amateur (1-3x/week) → basic rally capability
L2: Advanced Amateur (3-4x/week) → consistent technique
L3: Semi-Pro (5-6x/week) → automated technique under pressure
L4: Provincial/Pro → tactical execution
L5: National/World Class → complete tactical system
```

## 1.3 Training Workflow: L1→L2

**Goal**: Build basic technical framework in 4 weeks

**Training A: Forehand Clear Foundation**

| Step | Method | Volume | Rest | Standard |
|:-----|:-------|:------:|:----:|:---------|
| 1 | Fixed feed (same position) | 20 reps × 3 sets | 60s | 15/20 to back line |
| 2 | Moving feed (run from center) | 15 reps × 3 sets | 90s | 12/15 to back line |
| 3 | Self-check | - | - | Arm not sore after? |

**Training B: Basic Footwork**

| Week | Content | Volume | Standard |
|:----:|:--------|:------:|:---------|
| 1 | Single line (center→net) | 10×3 sets | 3 steps to arrive |
| 2 | Two lines (fore+backhand net) | 10 random×3 | Direction judgment <0.3s |
| 3 | Four lines (all corners) | 15 random×4 | All 3 steps |
| 4 | Assessment: center→back→net | <6s | Pass → enter L2 |

## 1.4 Training Workflow: L2→L3

**Goal**: Build complete technique library + automated footwork in 8 weeks

**Training: Clear-Drop-Attack Workflow**

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Fixed Pattern**
Coach feeds in fixed order:
1. Serve to forehand back → 2. You clear straight → 3. Coach returns to your backhand back → 4. You drop to opponent's backhand net → 5. Coach nets to your forehand net → 6. You net shot → end
- 1 cycle = 6 shots, 10 cycles = 1 set, 3 sets, rest 90s

**Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4): Semi-Random**
Coach feeds randomly (clears/drops/nets only). You don't know direction.
- 15 consecutive no-error = 1 set, 4 sets, rest 60s
- Standard: <3 errors/set

**Phase 3 (Weeks 5-8): Add Attack**
Coach feeds a "half-high" shuttle during rally — you must attack it
- 20 balls with 5 attack opportunities, 5 sets
- Standard: catch 80% of opportunities

# Chapter 2: Women's Singles

## Elite Profile Card

```
Modern WS has essentially merged with MS training methodology.
Key difference: female players have 50-60% of male upper body strength,
but 70-80% lower body strength and 85-95% aerobic capacity.
```

## Training Workflow: Defense-to-Attack

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2): Pure Defense**
- Coach smashes continuously (70% power)
- You receive and return to back court
- 15 reps × 4 sets, rest 60s. Standard: catch 12/15

**Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4): Defense + Redirect**
- After receiving smash, redirect to open court
- 15 reps × 4 sets. Standard: 8/15 to open court

**Phase 3 (Weeks 5-6): Defense → Attack**
- Receive smash → redirect → coach nets → you attack
- 12 reps × 4 sets. Standard: 6/12 complete transition

# Chapter 3: Men's Doubles

## Elite Profile Card

```
MD core abilities (ranked):
① Serve & receive (determines 60% of points)
② Rotation (attack↔defense transition)
③ Flat exchanges (mid-court drives)
```

## Training Workflow: Serve & Receive Patterns

**Week 1: Pattern A** — Serve short to forehand net → Receive push to backhand back → 3rd shot net kill
- 20 reps × 5 sets. Standard: 5 sets zero errors

**Week 2: Pattern B** — Serve short to backhand net → Receive cross-court net → Partner back court smash
- 20 reps × 5 sets

**Week 3: Pattern A+B Random** — Use eye/hand signals to decide pattern before serve
- 30 reps × 5 sets. Standard: 90% correct execution

**Week 4: Add Opponent Reaction** — Two opponents on the other side reacting to your patterns
- Match simulation, 20 points

# Chapter 4: Mixed Doubles

## Elite Profile Card

```
Golden division:
  Female: Net (blocks + drops + pushes)
  Male: Back court (smash + clears + large area defense)

XD core abilities:
① Serve & receive (determines opening)
② Female net ability (50% of scoring chances)
③ Rotation coordination
```

## Training Workflow: Female Net Blocking

**Phase 1: Fixed Block** — Male smashes fixed position → Female blocks fixed line
- 20 reps × 5 sets. Standard: 90% success (ball goes down over net)

**Phase 2: Random Block** — Male smashes random direction → Female adjusts
- 20 reps × 5 sets. Standard: 80% success

**Phase 3: Block + Continuity** — Block → immediately prepare for next ball, 10 consecutive
- 10 reps × 6 sets. Standard: don't lose balance during blocks

# Chapter 5: Women's Doubles

## Elite Profile Card

```
WD characteristics:
  • Less smash threat than MD → more rallies
  • Higher avg shots per point → more endurance needed

Key abilities:
① Serve & receive consistency
② Defensive ability (receiving smash + redirecting)
③ Smash & net coordination
```

## Training Workflow: Smash-Net Coordination

**Phase 1: Fixed** — A smashes → B blocks net (fixed line)
- 20 reps × 4 sets. Standard: A's smash lands in target zone, B's net shot goes down

**Phase 2: Variable** — A smashes random → B adjusts block direction
- 20 reps × 4 sets. Standard: B's block direction coordinates with A's smash

**Phase 3: Continuous Attack** — A smash → B block → opponent defends → A smashes again → B blocks again
- 3 consecutive cycles = 1 point, 10 pts × 3 sets. Standard: complete 3 cycles without error

# Chapter 6: Methodology — How to Apply Elite Methods to Your Situation

## The Four Questions

```
① What is my goal? (specific, measurable)
② What level am I at? (use the progression path)
③ What levels do I need to pass to reach my goal?
④ How many hours per week can I train?
```

## Environment Adaptation Formula

```
Your plan = Elite principles × Your resources ÷ Your constraints

Principles (fixed):
  ✓ Progressive difficulty
  ✓ Technique → Tactics → Match sequence
  ✓ Fitness matches match energy structure

Example: Office worker, 2x/week, no coach
  Session 1 (weekend): Technique 60min + Conditioning 30min
  Session 2 (weekend/work evening): Match 60min + Technique 30min
  Morning (15min daily): Footwork shadow swings

Not elite volume — but the best you can do NOW.
```
