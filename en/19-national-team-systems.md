## Chapter 1: China National Team

### 1.1 Progression Path (5 Levels)

```
L1: Sports School / Interest Class (ages 6-10)
  → 2-3x/week, 1.5hr/session
  → Cost: approx 50-100 RMB/session

L2: Provincial Sports School / Junior Team (ages 10-14)
  → 4-5x/week, 2-3hr/session
  → Selection: local competitions

L3: Provincial Team (ages 14-18)
  → Full-time training (6 days/week)
  → Entry: provincial championship top 3

L4: National Youth Team (ages 16-19)
  → Entry: National Junior Championship performance
  → Full-time, national training center

L5: National Team (ages 18+)
  → Entry: National Championship / trial matches
  → Full-time, Beijing/Hainan training base
```

### 1.2 Training Philosophy: "Three Stricts + High Volume"

```
① Strict technique standards
② Training difficulty > match difficulty
③ Everything serves match reality
④ Volume accumulates to quality change

Daily schedule:
  6:00-7:30 — Morning fitness
  9:00-12:00 — Technical training
  14:30-17:30 — Tactical/conditioning
  19:00-20:30 — Recovery + video review
  Total: 7-8 hours/day, 6 days/week
```

### 1.3 Key Training Methods

```
Drill 1: Four-Corner Multi-Shuttle
  - Feed speed: every 1.5-3 sec (by level)
  - Volume: 15-40 reps/set, 4-10 sets
  - Rest: 45-90 sec
  - Standard: 70-90% to target zone

Drill 2: Team Internal Competition
  Pressure higher than international matches
  Teammates know your weaknesses — forces improvement
```

## Chapter 2: China National Youth Team

### 2.1 Training Focus (differs from senior team)

| Dimension | Youth Team | Senior Team |
|:----------|:-----------|:------------|
| Technique % | 50% | 35% |
| Fitness % | 30% | 40% |
| Tactics % | 15% | 25% |
| Load | 60-70% of senior | 100% |

### 2.2 Key Principle for Youth

```
"No heavy weights before age 16."
  Bodyweight training only (pushups, pullups, jumps)
  Core strength > absolute strength
  Movement quality > training volume
```

### 2.3 The Dangerous Transition: Youth→Senior

```
>60% elimination rate.

Causes (not technical):
① Load adaptation failure (volume doubles overnight)
② Competition environment shock (best in youth → worst in senior)
③ Self-management failure (no coach watching you 24/7)
```

## Chapter 3: Japan — "Run Until You Can't" Philosophy

### 3.1 Training Method: 3-Person Rotation Multi-Shuttle

```
Setup:
  1 trainee on court + 2 feeders
  Feeder 1: 20 sec → Feeder 2: 20 sec → continuous
  Trainee: 40 sec non-stop, 15 sec rest

Parameters:
  Pace: 20% faster than fastest match rally
  Sets: 8-10 rounds = 1 block
  Total: 3 blocks per session
```

### 3.2 Kento Momota's "Instant Reaction" Training

```
Coach feeds flat/fast balls (not high clears)
Trainee stands at mid-court — minimal backswing
Target: ball-in to ball-out < 0.12 sec
Volume: 50 consecutive — speed cannot drop
```

## Chapter 4: Korea — Doubles Kingdom

### 4.1 Key Training: Half-Court Attack/Defense

```
Setup:
  2 players in one half court
  Must rotate after each shot (no 2 consecutive)
  Complete attack→defense→attack transition

Purpose:
  Rotation awareness
  Position adjustment
  Non-verbal communication
```

### 4.2 Korean Mixed Doubles Philosophy

```
"Female players must have back-court capability."
  — Otherwise opponents just target the female player's backhand
  Female players do same back-court training volume as males
```

## Chapter 5: France — INSEP Model

### 5.1 French vs Asian Training Differences

| Dimension | China | Japan | Korea | France |
|:----------|:------|:------|:-------|:-------|
| Daily hours | 6-8hr | 5-7hr | 5-6hr | 3-4hr |
| Tech % | 50% | 40% | 45% | 70% |
| Fitness % | 35% | 45% | 40% | 20% |
| Youth system | Sports school | School+club | Mixed | INSEP (edu+training) |

### 5.2 French "Thinking Player" Training

```
After every 10 balls — stop and answer 3 questions:
  1. What did I do right?
  2. What did I do wrong?
  3. What will I change next set?

Cannot answer → that set was wasted.
Coach does not answer for you — you must learn self-diagnosis.
```

## Chapter 6: Methodology — Adapt to Your Environment

### 6.1 Six Universal Principles

```
1. Professional training = specific + measurable + timed
2. Youth training = foundation before specialization
3. Physical training = matches match energy structure
4. Technique = automated before adding pressure
5. Recovery = part of training, not separate
6. Adapt elite methods to YOUR conditions
```

### 6.2 Environment Adaptation Formula

```
Your plan = Elite principle × Your resources ÷ Your constraints

Example: Office worker, 2x/week, no coach
  Training 1 (weekend):
    Technique 60min + Conditioning 30min
  Training 2 (weekday evening):
    Match play 60min + Technique 30min
  Morning (15min daily):
    Footwork shadow swings

This isn't elite volume — but it's the best you can do NOW.
As your time increases, the plan upgrades.
```
