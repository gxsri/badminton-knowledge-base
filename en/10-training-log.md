## III. Level 0 Stage Assessments

### 3.1 Baseline Test

Complete the following tests before starting Level 0 training:

| Test Item | Method | Baseline Data | Level 0 Target | Met? |
|:----------|:-------|:------------:|:--------------:|:----:|
| Single-leg stance (eyes open/sec) | 2 attempts per side, best taken | sec | 60 sec | □ |
| Single-leg stance (eyes closed/sec) | 2 attempts per side, best taken | sec | 20 sec | □ |
| Dead bug (correct reps) | No upper limit, until failure | reps | 20 reps | □ |
| Bird dog (correct reps) | No upper limit, until failure | reps | 16 reps | □ |
| Plank (sec) | Until failure | sec | 60 sec | □ |
| Single-leg squat (reps per side) | Controlled form until failure | reps | 8 reps | □ |
| Squat (reps) | Bodyweight until failure | reps | 30 reps | □ |
| Kneeling push-up (reps) | Until failure | reps | 20 reps | □ |

### 3.3 Level 0 Graduation Assessment

After completing the 4-week training, evaluate the following criteria:

| Assessment Dimension | Assessment Method | Passing Standard | Result |
|:--------------------|:-----------------|:---------------:|:------:|
| Neuromuscular Control | Single-leg squat stability | Knee does not cave in, pelvis stable | □ Pass □ Fail |
| Kinetic Chain Efficiency | Medicine ball throw (2kg) | Smooth full-body coordinated power transmission | □ Pass □ Fail |
| Basic Strength | Bodyweight squat | 30+ reps with proper form | □ Pass □ Fail |
| Core Endurance | Plank | 60+ seconds | □ Pass □ Fail |
| Mental Readiness | Self-assessment | Attention self-rating ≥ 6/10 | □ Pass □ Fail |
| Attendance | 4-week training record | At least 12 sessions completed | □ Pass □ Fail |

**Overall Assessment:**
- □ **All Passed** → May proceed to Level 1 training
- □ **Mostly Passed (4-5 items)** → May proceed to Level 1, but continue strengthening weak areas
- □ **Not Passed (≤3 items)** → Recommend repeating Level 0, focus on unpassed items

| Question | Score (1-10) | Notes |
|:---------|:----------:|:------|
| How is your sleep quality? | /10 | |
| How is your post-training fatigue recovery? | /10 | |
| How is your muscle soreness level? | /10 | (1=No pain 10=Cannot move) |
| Any joint discomfort? | /10 | (1=None 10=Severe) |
| How is your mental state? | /10 | |
| How is your training motivation? | /10 | |
| How regular is your diet? | /10 | |

**Scoring Rules:**
- Total > 50/70: Good condition, can train normally
- Total 35-50/70: Focus on recovery, reduce volume as appropriate
- Total < 35/70: Recommend rest or seek medical attention

## VI. Usage Instructions

1. **Daily Training Log** — Fill in after each training session, takes no more than 5 minutes
2. **Weekly Assessment** — Fill in every Sunday, review the past week's training (10 minutes)
3. **Level Stage Assessment** — Fill in at the start and end of each Level
4. **Monthly Summary** — Fill in at the end of each month (15 minutes)
5. **Physical Condition Self-Assessment** — Once per week, completed together with the weekly assessment

> **Tip**: These log templates can be printed out for use, or copied into a note-taking app. Consistent logging is the fastest path to progress.
