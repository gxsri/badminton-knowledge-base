# Warm-up & Cool-down Workflow

> **For**: All players and coaches  
> **Core idea**: Warm-up transitions your body from "daily mode" to "sports mode." Cool-down transitions it safely back.

# Chapter 1: Pre-Training/Match Warm-up

## Standard Warm-up (15 min) — Daily Training

```
Phase ①: Blood flow (3 min)
  Jogging / Jump rope / Jumping jacks
  Target HR: 120-130 bpm
  Signal: light sweat

Phase ②: Dynamic stretching (5 min)
  Ankle circles × 15 each
  Knee circles × 15 each
  Hip circles × 15 each
  Lunge + rotation (thoracic) × 8 each
  Shoulder circles × 15 each
  Neck side bend × 10s each

Phase ③: Neural activation (3 min)
  Fast feet (increasing tempo): 30s
  Agility ladder (single/double): × 2 each
  Split step practice: 10 reps
  Signal: feet feel "alive"

Phase ④: Sport-specific (4 min)
  Shadow swings × 10 (check power sequence)
  Half-court rally (clears): 15-20
  Half-court drops + net: 10
  Signal: can hit normal quality shots
```

## Match Day Warm-up (25 min)

```
Longer, includes match simulation + mental prep.

Same 4 phases as above but ×1.5 volume each.
Phase ④ extended to 8 min — includes full court rallies.

Before stepping on court:
  1 deep breath → say your "key word"
```

## Common Warm-up Mistakes

```
✗ Static stretching instead of dynamic (weakens muscles temporarily)
✗ Skipping warm-up ("I'll warm up during play")
✗ Warming up too long (fatigued before training starts)
```

# Chapter 2: Post-Training Cool-down

## Standard Cool-down (10 min)

```
Phase ①: Cool-down (2 min)
  Light jog/walk — bring HR below 100 bpm
  Don't sit or lie down immediately

Phase ②: Static stretching (5 min — hold each 20-30s)
  • Calf stretch (wall push)
  • Quad stretch (standing)
  • Hamstring stretch (seated forward fold)
  • Hip flexor stretch (lunge, knee down)
  • Thoracic rotation (side-lying)
  • Shoulder cross-body stretch
  • Forearm stretch

Phase ③: Foam rolling (3 min)
  • Calves × 30s
  • Quads × 30s
  • IT band × 30s
  • Glutes × 30s
  • Upper back × 30s
```

## Priority Muscles for Badminton Stretching

| Priority | Muscle | Why | Consequence if ignored |
|:--------:|:-------|:----|:----------------------|
| 1 | Calves | Most used in every shot | Achilles tendinitis |
| 2 | Hamstrings | Heavy in lunges/cutting | Hamstring strain |
| 3 | Hip flexors | Constant low stance | Anterior pelvic tilt → back pain |
| 4 | Chest | Racket sports tighten chest | Rounded shoulders → impingement |
| 5 | Forearm flexors | Constant gripping | Tennis/golfer's elbow |

# Chapter 3: Environmental Adjustments

```
Hot weather (>30°C):
  Warm-up: reduce to 10-12 min
  Hydrate during warm-up

Cold weather (<10°C):
  Warm-up: extend to 20-25 min
  Add high-knees/squat jumps in Phase ①
  Layer clothing, remove when warm

Air-conditioned indoor:
  Standard 15 min warm-up works
```

## Age Adjustments

```
Under 25: 10 min warm-up sufficient
25-35: 15 min standard
35-45: 15-20 min (extend dynamic stretching)
45+: 20+ min (add joint mobility work)
```
